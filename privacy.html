ARM Ring Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect. To provide rankings and progress sync, the game stores: a player name you choose, an anonymous account ID, and your game results (difficulty, position number, time, move count, date). If you choose to secure your account, we also store your e-mail address. Settings such as sound and theme are kept on your device.

How it is used. Only to run the game: showing your history, your best results and rankings against other players. Your player name and results are visible to other players on the leaderboard. We do not sell your data.

Where it is stored. On our database provider (Supabase) and locally on your device.

Advertising. If the game shows ads, the ad provider may use your device's advertising identifier according to its own privacy policy. You can reset or limit it in your device settings.

Your choices. You can change your player name at any time and delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). Deleting removes your profile, results and ranking permanently.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
